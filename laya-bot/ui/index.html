Cross-Game Empirical Discoveries
1. Shortlist & Noul Superiority
In both 8-Puzzle (3.0 steps) and 2048 (+53.5% score), Design A (independent Noul questions) crushed multiple-choice. Single choice dictionaries suffer from key order bias (`m0`) and reversible action traps. Independent truth probability queries break these traps completely!
2. Prompt Token Compaction
Across all games, compact factual representations (Design K: 157 tokens) outperformed verbose English prose (Design J: 450 tokens) by +41.0% in Tic-Tac-Toe and +37.0% in Connect Four. Diluted prompts distract the model's spatial attention.
3. The 1-Ply Tactical Ceiling
Against smart_random, Laya's win rate dropped to 16.0% in Tic-Tac-Toe and 2.0% in Connect Four. While Laya reliably detects winning moves against random opponents, single-turn probability models lack recursive lookahead without explicit tree search (e.g. minimax).
